Faz agora! is a free task manager without advertisements or tracking.

The app interface is currently available only in Brazilian Portuguese.

Create quick tasks or define a date and time. Organize plans with formatted descriptions and interactive subtasks. Configure daily, weekly, monthly, yearly or custom recurrence.

The application works offline. When you want to use more than one device, configure your own WebDAV server to synchronize the same task file between Android and Linux.

The support section connects to faz.whats.men to retrieve donation totals. If you choose to create a Pix payment, the selected amount and your email address are sent to create the payment, and the app checks its status. Your tasks are not sent to this service. An internet connection is required for these support features.

Your task data remains on the device or on the WebDAV server you choose. Faz agora! does not use telemetry, behavioral advertising or data sales.
